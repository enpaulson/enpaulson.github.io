About Me

Research & teaching

My research focuses on wireless communications, particularly LEO satellite networks, distributed optimization, and intelligent resource allocation for dynamic communication systems. At Hanyang University, I conduct research in the Department of Electronic Engineering and contribute to undergraduate and graduate teaching across Electronic Engineering and Hanyang YK Intercollege. My academic interests connect communication systems, mathematical foundations, and computational methods with practical engineering challenges. I also explore space–ground computing and intelligent sensing, linking theoretical methods with the design and evaluation of connected systems.

Research

Intelligent decisions.
Connected systems.

Resource coordination and online optimization for dynamic space–ground communication systems.

01 / Networks

LEO satellite connectivity

Exploring how moving constellations can coordinate communication resources as connectivity changes over time. Interests include multibeam access, beam alignment, sub-channel assignment, and spatial reuse.

02 / Optimization

Distributed online decisions

Developing distributed approaches to resource allocation and task offloading, including two-timescale primal–dual optimization and decisions based on local neighbor information.

03 / Computing

Space–ground task offloading

Studying the interaction between communication availability and computational workload, with an interest in architectures that support in-orbit computing.
